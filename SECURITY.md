# Security

tellem records what coding agents did, including transcripts, so a defect can
expose private material. Please report one privately.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting: open the repository's **Security**
tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Do not open a public issue, and do not
include a real secret in the report; describe its shape instead.

Include the tellem version, the harness and its version, what tellem wrote or
exposed and where, and the steps that reproduce it.

## Scope

In scope: a secret or private content that reaches a record unredacted, a record
written outside the scope tellem says it uses, a plan that applies without its
confirmation, and anything that lets one user read another's records.

Out of scope: what a harness itself stores in its own transcript files.

## Supported versions

Only the latest release receives fixes.
